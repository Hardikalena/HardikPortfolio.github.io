About Me

I'm a passionate game developer specializing in Unity and C#, focused on building polished, scalable, and engaging gameplay experiences.

I completed my BCA in Game Development from Ajeenkya DY Patil University with a CGPA of 8.50. I also gained hands-on industry experience as a Game Development Intern at Sherlock Studios, where I worked on refactoring legacy systems and implementing horror gameplay features.

My portfolio includes 2D arcade games, 3D survival experiences, VR projects, and modular gameplay systems such as combat, interaction, and traffic systems. I'm always eager to keep learning, improve my craft, and contribute to creative game development projects.
