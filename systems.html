Modular Combat System

A fully scalable and modular approach to sword combat in Unity, designed with SOLID principles and clean architecture. This system demonstrates advanced programming concepts including state machines, strategy patterns, and component-based design.

System Architecture
  • Modular Design: Component-based architecture for easy extension
  • Game Feel: Camera Shakes and Haptic Feedback for Controllers
  • Soulslike Pattern: Souls Like combat strategies and styles
  • Observer Pattern: Event-driven combat notifications
  • Scriptable Objects: Data-driven weapon configurations
Combat Features
  • Combo System: Chain attacks with timing-based combos
  • Parry & Block: Defensive mechanics with precise timing
  • Damage System: Critical hits and damage calculation
  • Animation Integration: Smooth animation blending
  • Weapon Variety: Multiple weapon types and behaviors

Smart Traffic System

A lightweight Unity traffic system built around waypoint-based vehicle AI, traffic light control, lane sensors, and modular intersection logic. Vehicles respond to signals, follow routes, stop smoothly, and navigate through intersections with scalable behavior suitable for prototypes and city simulation foundations.

System Architecture
  • Waypoint AI: Vehicles follow configurable route points with branching paths
  • Intersection Manager: Centralized control for opposite traffic signal groups
  • Sensor-Based Logic: Dedicated lane triggers manage stop, go, and traffic flow
  • Modular Design: Easy to expand with new roads, vehicles, and behaviors
  • Reusable Components: Clean Unity C# scripts for scalable traffic systems
Traffic Features
  • Traffic Lights: Dynamic red, yellow, and green signal control
  • Vehicle Spawning: Cars, buses, and trucks spawn with route assignment
  • Lane Detection: Vehicles react to road sensors and signal zones
  • Intersection Flow: Vehicles stop, wait, and proceed based on light state
  • Multi-Vehicle Support: Works with different vehicle types and road setups
